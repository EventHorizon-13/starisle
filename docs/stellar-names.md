# Stellar name supplement (2026-10-09)

Chinese designations and HIP identifiers were checked against the Stellarium 26.3 Chinese sky culture:
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/v26.3/skycultures/chinese/index.json

HIP 71683: 南门二 (A); HIP 39953: 天社一; HIP 41037: 海石一; HIP 33579: 弧矢七; HIP 100751: 孔雀十一; HIP 45238: 南船五.

The application supplement stores individual name/identifier facts and explicitly distinguishes Alpha Centauri A (HIP 71683) and B (HIP 71681). Proxima Centauri is HIP 70890; Barnard's Star is HIP 87937. Component aliases for Sirius A and Procyon A refer to the existing primary-star catalogue records. No generic removal of A/B suffixes is performed.
